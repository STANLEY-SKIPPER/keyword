# KeyWord

A typing test that helps beginners improve.

**Try it:** https://stanley-skipper.github.io/keyword/

Each test is a goal, such as typing 15 words in 30 seconds. Reach the goal and the next one gets a little harder; miss it and the next one gets a little easier. After each test, KeyWord shows your speed, your accuracy, a short tip, and a keyboard of the keys you missed. Click a missed key to practise it.

Results are saved in your own browser, so they stay private to you.
